<Lede>Baton hands out tickets to AI coding agents one at a time, keeps each claim alive with a
lease, and moves each ticket along its course from claim to merge.</Lede>

This wiki is part of the [Baton repository](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton). It holds
Baton's own tickets: what is planned, what is in progress, and what was done and why.

## Where to start

- [The roadmap](roadmap/index.mdx): every live ticket, grouped by status, with the next one to
  work on.
- [The archive](roadmap/archive/index.mdx): finished tickets.
- The code, the tests and the formal model are in the repository:
  [`src/`](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton/tree/main/src),
  [`test/`](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton/tree/main/test) and
  [`model/`](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton/tree/main/model). Its
  [README](https://github.com/immediately-run/baton#readme) explains the layout.

## Tickets

A ticket is one `.mdx` file under `roadmap/`, named after its id. Its frontmatter is what Baton
reads: `status`, `depends_on`, `order`, `tags`, `prs` and `repos`. The body is for people and
agents; Baton never stores it. `roadmap/item.schema.json` is the frontmatter's shape, and
`npm run check:wiki` checks every page against it.

- New tickets are numbered `B-1`, `B-2`, and so on.
- Tickets that moved here from the immediately.run docs roadmap keep their `R3-NNNN` ids.
- A finished ticket moves to `roadmap/archive/` with `status: done`.
